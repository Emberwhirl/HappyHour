# Email safe invite plan, 13 October 2026

Yu-Tian will paste an HTML invite into the source field of MedUni Wien Outlook. The hosted page and the paste snippet share one table. Images point at the live site so they resolve after deploy.

## Look

Keep the invitation palette of cream, gold, lilac and near black. Use a serif title, a short italic line, gold dividers, a framed poster, three fact cards, a fountain line, and a gold RSVP button.

## Mail limits

The layout is a table about 600 pixels wide. Styles sit on the elements. There is no script, no flex, no grid, no custom properties, no clamp, no AVIF and no srcset. Type uses Georgia with Times, and Arial with Segoe UI. A dark mode block repeats the same colours so a dark client keeps the cream text on the dark card. Rounded corners are limited to the button and the small lab mark, which stay square where a client ignores them.

## Facts

The words on the invite are Friday, 30 October 2026, 17:00 to 23:00 Vienna time, and CCR container. The fountain line is the one already on the site. The RSVP button opens the existing form with r=mail. A calendar file and a view on the web link use absolute addresses on the hosted site. The anime mascot stays, as the hosted PNG.

## Files

`email/invite.html` is the full page. `email/invite-paste.html` is only the snippet to paste. `email/poster-1200.jpg` is the poster at about 1200 pixels wide. `email/happy-hour.ics` is the six hour calendar file.
